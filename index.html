a USD viewer that tells you what it left out

lxg2it works

OpenUSD is a composition format: the file you pick is usually assembled from dozens of others, and a browser can only draw the parts it understands. Every viewer shows you something. This one also tells you what it could not show: which layers never arrived, which materials fell back to grey, which geometry was left out. Nothing is uploaded; the parsing runs in a worker on your machine.
